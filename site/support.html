Support

Blackboard Sync is a free app that downloads your course files from your school’s Blackboard Learn to your own device and keeps them up to date. It runs on macOS, Windows and iPad. The iPad app is released through the App Store; if you cannot find it there yet, it is still on its way. This page helps you get started and fix common problems.

Getting started

  1. Choose your school. Pick it from the list, or enter your school’s Blackboard address.
  2. Sign in on your school’s own Blackboard sign-in page, including two-step verification (MFA) if your school uses it. The app never sees or stores your password.
  3. Choose your courses. Select the term and the courses you want; you can change this later in the settings.
  4. Find your files. Downloads go into a University folder, organised by course.
    • Mac and Windows: in your Documents folder by default; you can pick another folder in the settings.
    • iPad: in the Files app, under Blackboard Sync.

Questions and troubleshooting

It says my sign-in expired, or asks me to “Sign in again”

Blackboard sessions expire after a while. Choose Sign in again, finish your school’s sign-in and MFA, and syncing continues where it left off. Your files and settings are kept.

My files are not appearing

  • Check that the course is selected in the settings and that you are signed in.
  • Start a sync by hand and wait for it to finish; the first sync of a large course can take a while.
  • Make sure the right term is chosen, and that your instructor has actually published the files in Blackboard.
  • Look in the right place: the University folder on Mac and Windows, or Files → Blackboard Sync on iPad.

Some files were skipped

To save space and data, the app skips audio and video files and any file larger than 500 MB. Open those in Blackboard directly.

My school is not in the list

Enter your school’s Blackboard Learn address by hand. If it still does not work, or you would like your school added to the list, open an issue on GitHub with your school’s name and Blackboard address.

How do I update the app?

  • macOS and Windows Setup builds: the app checks for updates and tells you when one is ready.
  • Windows, Microsoft Store build: the Microsoft Store delivers updates.
  • iPad: the App Store delivers updates.

Google Calendar sync

Google Calendar sync is optional and off until you connect it. It puts your deadlines on a separate “Blackboard Deadlines” calendar and never touches your main calendar. Disconnect any time in the app’s settings. Details are on the Privacy page.

On iPad

  • Opening files: open the Files app and go to Blackboard Sync, or open a file from inside the app.
  • GoodNotes, Notability and other apps: open a file, tap Share, and choose Open in to send it to your note-taking app.
  • Orientation: the iPad app works in landscape only, so turn your iPad sideways.

Deleting your account and data

  • Mac and Windows: the app keeps its data only on your device. Sign out to remove your sign-in, then uninstall the app to remove the rest. The Privacy page lists exactly what is removed and where it lives.
  • iPad: deleting the app removes its local files from your iPad. If you created a Blackboard Sync account in the iPad app, you can delete it in the app under Settings → Delete account.

Contact

Still stuck? Ask on GitHub Issues. Please say which device and app version you use and what you see, and leave out passwords and personal details. See also the Privacy Policy.
