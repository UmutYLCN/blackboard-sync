Download. Install. Sign in.

Getting started is that easy.

01 / macOS

Open the .dmg and drag the app into the Applications folder.

02 / Windows

Run Setup.exe. If SmartScreen warns you, choose More info → Run anyway.

On first launch choose your school’s address and the folder to save files in, then Sign in. The app is in English or Turkish; pick one in Settings.

Your password stays with you.

You type your password only into your school’s own sign-in page; the app never sees it and never stores it. The saved sign-in session stays on your computer and is sent only to your school’s Blackboard server. Optional Google Calendar shares deadline details with Google; see our privacy policy.
