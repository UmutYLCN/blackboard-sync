## Description

```text
Automatically download Blackboard course files to your PC, organised by term and course. The built-in directory covers 193 institutions with verified Blackboard Learn addresses, including 34 in the UK, 34 in the US and 27 in Saudi Arabia. Start typing your school’s name, abbreviation or domain to see suggestions. If it isn’t listed, enter its Blackboard address and sign in with your school account.

Use the AI subscription you already have. From Home, one click launches Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Codex (ChatGPT) or Antigravity in your current term folder. Install your preferred tool and sign in with your own account.

Connect Gemini Notebook (NotebookLM) from Home to export supported course files to one notebook per course. The optional export uses an unofficial connection to Google that may stop working if Google changes the service.

See assignment deadlines and course announcements in one window. Colour-coded course badges make it easier to tell which course a deadline, announcement or recent download belongs to.

Choose which courses sync and keep downloads running from the Windows notification area after closing the window.

Available in English, Türkçe, Español, Français, Deutsch and العربية.

blackboard-sync is an independent app and is not affiliated with Anthology Inc. or Blackboard.
```
