# Blackboard Sync 1.7.1

- **Simple latest-term rule & original term names.** The app automatically syncs your active semester using the university's authentic term names as written, without forced translations (such as "Fall" to "Güz") or artificial folder nesting.
- **First-run course confirmation.** Shows detected term and courses on a clear review card before the first download at schools where term dates are ambiguous or customized.
- **143 verified Blackboard schools.** The school picker dropdown is expanded from 16 to 143 verified universities worldwide with instant address lookup.
- **Resilient background re-sign-in.** Silent re-sign-in adapts to multi-provider SSO login flows with page stillness detection and fast-path credential checks.
- **Tidy folder structure & UI.** No empty folders are created for courses without files; cleaned up the Home sidebar by centering the title next to the logo and removing the deprecated past-term section.
- **NotebookLM on Windows & Store assets.** Full NotebookLM sync card parity on Windows with duplicate detection, plus bilingual (TR/EN) Microsoft Store assets.
