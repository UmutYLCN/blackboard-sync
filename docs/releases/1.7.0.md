# Blackboard Sync 1.7.0

- **NotebookLM on macOS.** Connect from the new **NotebookLM** card on Home, and after each Blackboard sync your downloaded course files and PDF notes are added to one notebook per course. **Sync NotebookLM** runs on demand, **Open NotebookLM** opens your notebooks in the browser, and Settings → NotebookLM holds the automatic toggle and Disconnect. Uses an unofficial connection to Google; your session stays on your Mac.
- **Never adds a file that is already in the notebook.** Existing sources are matched by the app's record, content, relative path or file name, and ambiguous matches are held back instead of uploaded twice. **Settings → NotebookLM → Find duplicates** lists extra copies and lets you confirm removing newer identical copies the app added.
- **Automatic re-sign-in in the background (macOS).** While the app runs, it keeps your Blackboard session alive and, when it expires, signs in again silently through your school's single sign-on without opening a window. If your school asks for a password or two-factor code, nothing is filled in and you get the usual **Sign in** notification. Controlled by Settings → Sync → **Automatic re-sign-in**.
