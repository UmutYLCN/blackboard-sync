# Blackboard Sync 1.6.2

- **A new main window on Windows.** Home, Sync activity, Announcements, Courses, Calendar and Settings are redrawn with Microsoft Edge WebView2: icons in the sidebar, colored file-type icons, rounded cards and dialogs that match the rest of the window.
- **Light or dark, your choice (Windows).** Settings → Appearance → Theme: **System** follows Windows, **Light** and **Dark** keep one. The change applies at once, title bar included.
- **Google Calendar on Windows.** Connect, sync now and disconnect from Settings → Google Calendar, as on macOS.
- **The app's logo in the tray.** The icon next to the clock is now the Blackboard Sync logo, with a colored dot while syncing, paused, signed out or after an error.
- Home's greeting writes a name Blackboard sends in capitals in normal case ("Good evening, Umut").
- On a computer without the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime the previous window opens instead.
